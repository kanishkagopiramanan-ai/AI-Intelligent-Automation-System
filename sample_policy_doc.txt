APEX ASSURANCE GROUP - MASTER LIFE & HEALTH POLICY AGREEMENT
POLICY DOCUMENT REF: AAG-POL-2024-8891
================================================================================

1. POLICY COVERAGE PERIOD AND TERM
The policy coverage period commences on the Date of Inception as specified in the Policy Schedule and remains in force for a baseline policy term of 15 years, subject to regular premium payment. Annual renewable terms apply to supplemental critical illness riders. All coverage guarantees remain valid provided the insured person complies with the disclosure norms of utmost good faith.

2. GRACE PERIOD FOR OVERDUE PREMIUM PAYMENTS
A grace period of thirty (30) calendar days is allowed for payment of overdue premiums for annual, semi-annual, and quarterly frequencies. For monthly payment frequencies via auto-debit, a grace period of fifteen (15) calendar days is granted. During this grace period, the policy shall remain in full force and effect without any loss of coverage. If a valid death or hospitalization claim arises during the grace period, the claim shall be honoured after deducting any unpaid premium installments.

3. FREE-LOOK CANCELLATION PERIOD
The policyholder is entitled to a free-look period of fifteen (15) calendar days from the date of physical receipt of the policy document (or thirty calendar days in case of policies sourced through distance marketing or electronic modes). During this period, the customer may review policy terms and conditions. If dissatisfied, the policyholder may return the policy stating the reason for objection. The company will refund the full premium paid after deducting proportionate risk premium for the period on cover, medical examination expenses incurred, and applicable stamp duty.

4. CLAIM SETTLEMENT CHECKLIST AND MANDATORY DOCUMENTS
To initiate claim settlement, the claimant must submit:
a) Duly completed and signed Claim Intimation Form.
b) Original Policy Certificate.
c) Attested copy of Death Certificate / Hospital Discharge Summary.
d) Complete medical records, indoor case papers, and diagnostic reports.
e) KYC documents of the nominee/claimant including PAN card and verified bank account passbook.
f) Police FIR and Final Police Investigation Report (mandatory in cases of accidental death or unnatural demise).

5. CLAIM SETTLEMENT TIMELINE AND GUARANTEES
Upon receipt of all requisite documents and complete clarifications, the insurer guarantees claim adjudication within thirty (30) calendar days. If an investigation is warranted regarding non-disclosure or early death claims within three years of inception, the investigation shall be completed within ninety (90) calendar days, and settlement finalized within thirty days thereafter.

6. PRE-EXISTING MEDICAL CONDITIONS AND WAITING PERIOD
Pre-existing conditions (defined as any medical ailment, illness, or injury diagnosed or treated within 48 months prior to the policy inception date) are subject to a mandatory waiting period of thirty-six (36) months of continuous coverage. No claims arising directly from or attributable to pre-existing conditions shall be payable before the expiration of the 36-month waiting period. After completion of 36 months of continuous renewals, pre-existing conditions become eligible for full coverage under standard terms.
