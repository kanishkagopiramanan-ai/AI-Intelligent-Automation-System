================================================================================
APEX ASSURANCE - FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS & CUSTOMER SERVICE CHARTER
================================================================================

1. PREMIUM PAYMENT MODES
Customers can remit premiums using Net Banking, Debit/Credit cards, UPI, or through NACH / ECS automated bank clearing mandates. Automatic recurring debits process on the 5th of each month for monthly billing cycles.

2. SURRENDER VALUE AND POLICY LOANS
Policies acquire a Guaranteed Surrender Value after three consecutive years of full premium payments. Policy loans are accessible up to 80% of the accrued surrender value at an annual simple interest rate of 8.5%.

3. NOMINEE CHANGE AND ASSIGNMENT
Policyholders can register or change their nominee at any point during the policy tenure by submitting Form NOM-01 accompanied by proof of identity of the nominee. Absolute assignment or conditional assignment requires written notice to the insurer along with the original contract.

4. GRIEVANCE REDRESSAL ESCALATION MATRIX
If a grievance is unresolved by the branch manager within 15 working days, policyholders may escalate the matter to the Chief Grievance Officer at grievance@apexassurance.example.com or approach the Insurance Ombudsman within their jurisdiction under the Insurance Ombudsman Rules.
